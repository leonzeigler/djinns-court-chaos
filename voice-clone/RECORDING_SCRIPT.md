# Judge Djinn Ra — Voice Recording Script

**Goal:** 1–3 minutes of YOUR voice so the game can clone it and make the judge sound like you.

**How to record:**
1. iPhone/Android: open your voice recorder app (Voice Memos on iPhone, Recorder on Android).
2. Hold the phone ~6 inches from your mouth, in a quiet room (no TV, no fan).
3. Read the lines below **like you're the judge** — big energy, commanding, have fun with it. Laugh where it says [laughs].
4. Record at least **1 full minute** (all of it is better — about 2 minutes).
5. Send the audio file here in chat (as an attachment).

**Don't worry about mistakes** — just keep going. Energy matters more than perfection.

---

## THE SCRIPT — read it like you mean it

Order in the court! Order in the court! Judge Djinn Ra is now presiding, and I don't wanna hear another word unless I call on you.

Plaintiff, step forward and state your case. And make it good, because my gavel is getting heavy.

[laughs] Oh, that's rich. That's the funniest thing I heard all week, and I heard a man try to pay child support in lottery tickets.

Defendant! You had ONE job. One! And you still messed it up. Explain yourself before I hold you in contempt of this courtroom.

Let the record show: the plaintiff came correct, the defendant came with excuses, and excuses don't pay the bills.

I've heard enough. Both sides argued, both sides sweated, but only one side told the truth — and it wasn't the side I expected.

Court is now in session, round two! The energy is high, the arguments are wild, and somebody in here is lying through their teeth.

Bailiff! Escort that weak argument straight out of my courtroom. We don't do fairy tales in here.

You want justice? Justice is a full-time job, and today — today I'm working overtime.

[laughs] Guilty! Guilty as charged! Somebody get this man a trophy for the worst excuse of the night.

Now listen close, because I'm only saying this once: in Djinn's courtroom, the truth always comes out. It might come out messy, but it comes out.

Court is adjourned! Djinn Ra declares the champions of the night! Justice has been served — gold style. Your wish... was my command.
